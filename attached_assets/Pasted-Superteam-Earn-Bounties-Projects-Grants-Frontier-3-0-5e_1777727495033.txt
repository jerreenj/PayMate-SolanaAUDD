Build real-world finance with AUDD on Solana
We’re looking for Solana builders to create practical, production-ready applications powered by AUDD stablecoin, Australia’s digital dollar infrastructure.

AUDD is building the foundation for a fully backed, compliant Australian dollar stablecoin designed for real-world digital finance. From payments and settlement to treasury and programmable money, our goal is to make AUDD the default AUD asset for on-chain use cases in Australia. While this is a grant for the Australian Digital Dollar, anyone in the world can apply.   

If you’re a Solana developer with a strong product instinct and want to build something useful, this grant is for you.

What you’ll build
We want teams or solo builders to develop a live Solana-based application, integration, or service that uses AUDD in a meaningful way.

This could include use cases such as:

payments
on-chain settlement
treasury management
programmable finance
merchant tools
consumer or business-facing financial apps
The key is simple: build something people can actually use.

What we’re looking for
Your project should:

use AUDD as the on-chain Australian dollar stablecoin
solve a real problem or unlock a clear user benefit
be technically sound and feasible on Solana
deliver a smooth, intuitive user experience
be capable of shipping in a production-ready form
Who should apply
You’re a strong fit if you have:

experience building on Solana
a solid understanding of stablecoins and on-chain finance
the ability to ship polished, reliable products
familiarity with payments, settlement, treasury, or financial primitives
a portfolio of previous Solana work
How to apply
Send us:

a short proposal outlining your AUDD use case
how you plan to build it on Solana
links to relevant past work or portfolio
a few lines on why you’re the right builder for this
your quote in AUDD
Evaluation criteria
We’ll assess applications based on:

strength and originality of the use case
technical feasibility
quality of previous work
understanding of AUDD and its potential applications
overall quality of the proposal
Grant amount
Funding ranges from AUDD 1,000 to AUDD 10,000, depending on the scope and potential impact of the project.

Why this matters
We think Australian dollar stablecoin infrastructure will unlock a new layer of digital finance. SolAUDD is a chance to build early, shape real use cases, and help define how AUDD moves on-chain.

If that sounds like your kind of build, apply.
